Who I Am

Who I Am

I am not the State of Hawaii. I am not the City and County of Honolulu. I am not affiliated with any government agency.

My Sources

What I Review

I review the State of Hawaii Unclaimed Property database and City and County of Honolulu escheated-check records for additional funds. These are two separate public databases — a person or business may appear in one, both, or neither.

Meet Me

Meet Me

James Hyde

Sole Operator, Hawaii Unclaimed Funds LLC

I started Hawaii Unclaimed Funds LLC after recognizing that many Hawaii residents and businesses may have money sitting in public records without knowing it is there.

Before starting this work, I attended Kapiʻolani Community College for culinary arts and built a career in hospitality and food service. I worked as a chef at places including Sheraton Waikiki, Bloomingdale's, and Waikiki Market. That background taught me the importance of organization, follow-through, attention to detail, and taking care of people behind the scenes.

After my partner and I welcomed our son, who needs extra care and support, I began looking for meaningful work I could build from home while staying available for my family. During that search, I saw an opportunity to help people identify and recover funds they may not realize are owed to them.

I created Hawaii Unclaimed Funds LLC to provide a transparent, respectful service for people and businesses listed in public unclaimed-property and escheated-check records. My goal is simple: help rightful owners understand what was found, verify the record for themselves, and decide whether they want my assistance with the recovery process.

I believe every person should be able to verify records independently. Hawaii Unclaimed Funds LLC is a private recovery assistance service, not a government agency, and anyone may pursue claims directly through the appropriate government office at no cost. I personally handle every claim — there's no call center and no case gets handed off to someone else.

Pricing

My Fee

My fee is charged only if recovery is successful and only after the client receives funds. There are no upfront fees. The full terms are set out in a written Recovery Agreement — the same document referenced in my Terms of Service — which discloses the record identified, my fee, and your right to cancel before you sign anything. You may also pursue claims directly through the appropriate government office at no cost — search the State of Hawaii Unclaimed Property database at unclaimedproperty.ehawaii.gov or Honolulu escheated-check records at Honolulu.gov. Fee agreements may be subject to applicable law. This website is for informational purposes only and is not legal advice.

Is This Right for You?

When Professional Help May Be Worth It

My service covers both the State of Hawaii Unclaimed Property database and Honolulu escheated checks. Here's when people find professional assistance most useful.

📋

Estate & Heir Claims

Claims involving a deceased relative require specific legal documentation. I handle these frequently for both State unclaimed property and Honolulu escheated checks.

🏢

Business Claims

Business or organizational claims have additional documentation requirements, including EIN and authorized signatory proof. I handle claims from both sources.

📍

Old or Missing Addresses

Claims with outdated addresses or missing documentation are common sources of delays and denials.

👥

Multiple Claimants

Claims involving multiple heirs or disputed ownership require careful coordination and documentation.

🔄

Denied or Delayed Claims

If your claim was denied or has stalled, I can review what happened and help with a corrected resubmission.

😌

Full-Service Management

Anyone who wants help managing the process from start to finish — so you don't have to chase anyone on your own.

Client Intake

Intake Support

My intake system may collect basic information and help schedule a follow-up. Sensitive documents should not be shared by phone or through the general contact form. I personally review every inquiry and follow up myself.
