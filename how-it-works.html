The Full Process

Step-by-Step: How I Recover Your Funds

1

Record Review

My lead source is the State of Hawaii Unclaimed Property database, publicly available at unclaimedproperty.ehawaii.gov. I search by personal name and business name.

You can do this yourself for free. The database is publicly accessible. I encourage you to verify any record directly before contacting me.
2

Free Consultation

You contact me by phone, email, or through my contact form. I'll review what was found in the State unclaimed property database, and explain exactly what's involved in pursuing the claim for your specific situation. This consultation is completely free and carries zero obligation. My intake system may collect basic information and help schedule a follow-up.

No pressure, ever. I'll tell you honestly if your situation is straightforward enough to handle yourself without hiring me.
3

Signed Recovery Agreement

If you'd like to proceed, I'll send you a Recovery Agreement for your electronic signature. The Recovery Agreement discloses:

  • The exact record(s) I've identified and where they appear
  • My contingency fee terms
  • The full terms of my engagement
  • Your right to cancel within a specified period

Fee agreements may be subject to applicable law. You sign digitally from any device — no printing, no office visit, no notary required for this step.

4

Document Collection

I'll provide you with a clear checklist of the exact documents needed for your specific claim type. Documents are returned to me by mail. Common documents include:

  • Government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport)
  • Social Security card or number verification
  • Proof of address matching the property record
  • For estate claims: death certificate, probate documents
  • For trust claims: trust instrument or certificate of trust
  • For business claims: business license, EIN documentation
5

Notarization

Some claims require notarized documents. I tell you exactly which pages need notarizing and what to bring, then you visit any Hawaii notary. I handle everything else.

6

Claim Preparation

I prepare a complete, properly formatted claim package to submit to the Hawaii Department of Budget and Finance. I review all documentation for accuracy and completeness before submission.

7

Claim Submission

I mail your complete claim package to the appropriate government office. I retain a complete copy of everything submitted for your records and mine.

8

Review & Follow-Up

The government office reviews the claim. Processing times vary by claim type and workload, but typically range from 6 weeks to 4 months for standard claims, and longer for complex estate, trust, or business claims. I check in when it makes sense. If the office requests anything, I handle it.

If the State Contacts You: If the Unclaimed Property office calls you about additional paperwork or questions, call me. I will handle the response. You are never left to figure it out alone.
9

Approval & Payment to You

When the claim is approved, payment is issued directly to you — the rightful owner. This comes in the form of a check or direct deposit from the appropriate government office. You receive the funds, not me. I never receive or hold your funds at any point.

10

My Fee — Only After You're Paid

Only after you receive your funds does my contingency fee apply. If the claim is denied or you receive nothing, you owe me nothing. Fee agreements may be subject to applicable law. This is my unconditional guarantee — my success depends entirely on your success.

My fee is charged only after successful recovery. You may also pursue claims directly through the appropriate government office at no cost.
