Why you received a letter from me

If you received a letter, call, email, postcard or text from me, it is most likely because your name, or the name of a family member, appears in the State of Hawaii unclaimed property database.

You don’t need to take my word for it. You can verify the record directly with the State’s own search tool.

How to search the State database

  • 1. Open the State’s search

    The State of Hawaii unclaimed property search, on the State’s own website.

  • 2. Search by name

    Search by a personal name or a business name. Try a relative’s name too.

  • 3. Find the record

    This database is the source of most of the letters I send.

Property types in the State’s records

  • Bank accounts

    Dormant checking and savings accounts, certificates of deposit (CDs) and safe deposit box contents.

  • Uncashed checks

    Uncashed checks, including payroll checks.

  • Insurance

    Life insurance proceeds.

  • Deposits

    Utility and rental security deposits.

  • Investments

    Stocks, dividends and brokerage accounts.
