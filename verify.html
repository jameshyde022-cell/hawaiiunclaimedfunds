How to Verify

Verify It Yourself — It's Free

Check State of Hawaii Unclaimed Property

The State of Hawaii maintains an unclaimed-property database. You can search by personal name or business name. This is the source of most of the letters I send. You can search and file directly with the State for free.

This database covers a broad range of property types including dormant bank accounts, insurance proceeds, utility deposits, and other abandoned assets.
