Hello, my name is James.

I audit Hawaii public records looking for unclaimed funds. The State lists the claims’ owners, and that’s where I start. Whether it’s a business, trust or estate, I attempt to locate the owner by referencing their name or reported address.

Many of these claims are 20–30 years old, and the addresses and phone numbers changed long ago. Sadly, but quite often, the claim owners have passed away and their families have no knowledge of these accounts.

It’s hard to know who to trust, and when I contact the family, they have every right to be skeptical. I am as transparent as possible, and I encourage everyone to verify for themselves.

Verify, and then give me a call. Ask me any questions you have, and then let me help you file a claim. This is your money. It doesn’t belong in a State trust account.

Usually, I can complete your paperwork within a week. I’ll let you know which documents the State will need from you to prove your identity and why you are entitled to claim these funds. A successful claim gets mailed to you. You pay nothing until you get paid.

What I bring to your claim

How I help you. The State’s verification process can quickly become complicated, frustrating and slow. I take the weight off your shoulders and help get your claim right the first time.

  • Experience that prevents mistakes

    I handle these claims regularly, so I know what examiners look for and what gets a file sent back.

  • State requirements, anticipated

    Old addresses, name changes, businesses and estates each need their own proof. I know the small things the State asks for that are not listed online.

  • Less chance of delays

    A complete, accurate claim package from day one keeps your file moving without back-and-forth.

  • No stress or guesswork

    No unfamiliar forms and no guessing which documents the State wants. I fill out the forms, give you a checklist of exactly what is required, then mail you the completed claim, ready for your signature and a notary seal.

  • No out-of-pocket risk

    Nothing upfront. I am paid only when your claim is approved and you receive your money.
