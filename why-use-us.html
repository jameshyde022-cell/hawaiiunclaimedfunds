Addressing the Obvious Question

"Why Would I Pay a Fee When I Can File for Free?"

This is exactly the right question to ask, and I respect you for asking it. The honest answer: for some people, you shouldn't hire me.

👍

When to File Yourself

  • Your claim is straightforward — matching name, current address, active ID
  • The amount is small (a few hundred dollars)
  • You have time, patience, and organizational skills
  • No estate, trust, probate, or multiple-claimant situations
  • You're comfortable with government paperwork
See the Free Verification Guide
🤝

When to Hire Me

  • Complex estate or trust claim (deceased relative's funds)
  • Business or corporate claim requiring EIN verification
  • Multiple claimants or heir disputes
  • Large amounts where getting it right really matters
  • Previous claim was denied or delayed
  • You simply prefer someone else to handle it

Why Clients Choose Me

8 Reasons People Hire a Professional

When people choose to hire me for State unclaimed property recovery, these are the most common reasons.

📋

Documentation Expertise

I know exactly what's required for each claim type — individual, estate, trust, business — and how to present it correctly. The wrong document or a missing detail can delay or deny a claim by months.

⏱️

Time & Convenience

The claim process can take dozens of hours: researching requirements, gathering documents, completing forms, following up. My clients pay my fee to get that time back. For many, that's an excellent trade.

👨‍👩‍👧

Estate & Heir Claims

Claiming funds on behalf of a deceased person is significantly more complex. It requires legal documentation proving you are an authorized heir or representative.

🏢

Business Claims

If your business has State unclaimed property, you need to prove authorized signatory status, provide EIN documentation, and potentially corporate resolutions. I handle business claims regularly.

🔄

Persistence & Follow-Up

Without professional follow-up, claims can stall without explanation. I proactively monitor status, respond to inquiries immediately, and escalate when appropriate.

🔍

Comprehensive Search

I search the State of Hawaii Unclaimed Property database thoroughly, including alternative name spellings, former addresses, maiden names, and related business entities. Clients frequently discover additional funds they didn't know about.

❌

Prior Denial or Delay

If your previous claim was denied or has been stalled, I can review what happened, identify the issue, and resubmit with the correct documentation.

😌

Peace of Mind

Sometimes the value is simply knowing a professional is handling something important. If you have a large claim or are going through a difficult life event (estate, divorce, medical issues), not having to manage this paperwork is genuinely valuable.
