Why work with me to file your claim?

Filing on your own is possible, but the State’s verification process can get complicated and slow. I build a complete claim package from day one, so your file keeps moving.

How I trace the owners of unclaimed property. The research comes before the letter.

  • Business and corporate records

    DCCA filings and business registries, to find the owners or officers of dissolved or relocated companies.

  • Address and skip-tracing searches

    Old addresses, property records and public data, to find people who have moved.

  • Genealogy and heir research

    Family trees and estate records, to find the heirs when the owner has died.

  • Claim paperwork

    Completed claim forms and a checklist of what the State requires, ready for your signature.

Estate claims for heirs, executors and trustees

Many of my letters go to the family of an owner who has died.

Ask about estates

Who can claim

Usually the heirs, the estate, or the successor trustee when the property sat in a trust.

What the State asks for

A death certificate, proof of your relationship, and proof you may act: probate letters, an affidavit or a Certificate of Trust.

Why estate claims take longer

The State checks the owner has died and you are authorised. I tell you which papers your claim needs.

The ones people ask on the first call.

Read all questions

Why did I get a letter about unclaimed property?

Your name, or a relative’s, appears in the State of Hawaii unclaimed property records. You can check that yourself on the State’s site.

Are you part of the State of Hawaii?

No. Hawaii Unclaimed Funds LLC is a private service I run. I am not affiliated with the State.

Can I file a claim myself?

The State lets owners file directly. Many people hire me because proving ownership gets complicated, and a missing document can stall a claim for months.

How is your claim fee paid?

A contingency fee, charged only after the State pays you. If the claim fails, you owe nothing. Fee agreements may be subject to applicable law.

How long does a claim take?

Often several months, since the State reviews each claim. I follow up on yours so you do not have to.
