Helpful Links

Official State of Hawaii sources you may need for an unclaimed property claim. All free to use.

🏛️ Search State Unclaimed Property The State of Hawaii's official database. Look up any personal, business, or trust name for free. unclaimedproperty.ehawaii.gov 📄 Order a Certified Death Certificate Hawaii Department of Health, Vital Records. Required for most claims where the owner has passed away. health.hawaii.gov ⚖️ Small-Estate Affidavit (Form 3CE210) Hawaii State Judiciary's Affidavit for Collection of Personal Property of the Decedent. Used to claim a small estate without full probate. PDF. courts.state.hi.us
